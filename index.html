What it does

Everything you need to diagnose a bundle

Upload once. Nexara parses the whole support archive and organizes it into structured, drill-down views — no scripts, no external services.

Automated indicators

40+ system, traffic, and network indicators run against every bundle — each with detection logic, thresholds, evidence, and corrective actions.

Critical & Deep Insights

A dashboard surfaces the issues that matter first, then Deep Insights lists every finding sorted by severity with expandable evidence.

Debug & RCA

Session debug with pattern-based log + PCAP analysis, deterministic crash stack-trace RCA, and optional LLM-powered deep root-cause analysis.

Performance metrics

CPU, memory, packet processing, and top-process charts over time — with zoom and per-node filters for HA deployments.

Parsed command views

Sessions, Interfaces, Services, Alarms, Events, FIB, Routing, and BGP — core show commands rendered as clean, searchable tables.

Local & private

Runs entirely on your machine in the browser with no external dependencies. Bundles never leave your computer — AI features are optional.

Two pipelines, one tool

SSR/128T and SRX, fully separated

Nexara auto-detects the platform from your upload and switches to the right analysis pipeline. Indicators never cross platforms.

SSR / 128T Router TSI bundle

Upload a Tech Support Info .zip for full automated analysis across all indicators.

  • System: version, cores, memory, processes, coredumps, NTP, HA symmetry
  • Traffic: adaptive encryption, TCP state, session installs, congestion
  • Network: peer paths, source-NAT, routing parity, DHCP, IPSec

SRX Firewall RSI output

Upload the RSI output, a /var/log archive, and/or config. Chassis clusters parsed from a single RSI.

  • Chassis cluster: RG priority, split brain, failover, control/fabric links
  • Flow sessions, source-NAT, SPU/PFE performance, RE health
  • Alarms, interfaces, IPsec VPN, config audit, log scan
How it works

From bundle to findings in three steps

1

Install & launch

Download Nexara, then start it. Open localhost:8443 in your browser — everything runs locally.

2

Drop your bundle

Drag a .zip TSI or RSI bundle onto the upload area. Upload both HA node bundles together for side-by-side analysis.

3

Read the findings

Nexara runs every indicator and presents critical insights, evidence, charts, and corrective actions across structured views.
